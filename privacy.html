Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The Business Builder ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Assessment requests: your name, email, phone, business details, online profiles and anything else you choose to share in the form.
  • Quiz and scorecard answers: these stay in your browser unless you carry them over into the assessment form and send it.

How we use it

We use your information only to prepare your assessment, contact you about it and, if you become a client, provide our services. We do not sell or rent your information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run this website and receive form submissions (such as our email and form providers), and only as needed for those services. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads. If we add analytics or advertising tools, we'll update this page.

How long we keep it

We keep assessment requests for as long as needed to respond and for reasonable business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information we have about you, or to stop hearing from us, email andrew@thebusinessbuilder.tech.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
